P1: Stochastic kernels share process-global RNG in step execution; module composition changes an independent module's trajectory.
References: starsim/solvers.py:2277 seeds kn.seed once during packing; starsim/kernels.py:978 and stochastic draws use np.random; whole-run explicitly seeds at kernels.py:1136. design/11-random.md:9-23 promises independent streams by full declaration path.
Verified with NUMBA_DISABLE_JIT=1 (normal-JIT verification pending): Disease(recovery=progression('I -> R',rate=.2), init=dict(I=100),name='ab'); Sim(...,solver='binomial',unit='day',stop=8,total_pop=100,verbose=0).run(profile=True) gives I=[100,86,75,65,54,41,35,30,25]. Adding independent identical disease named ba changes ab to [100,86,75,62,52,43,31,21,17]; reordering the two modules swaps their trajectories. Profiling is supposed to observe execution; optimization eligibility or profile should never alter its randomness contract. Use explicit engine RNG state in kernels or named counter-based streams; test alone/together/reordered and whole/step/profile paths.

P1: Engine seed is sum(ord(c) for c in module.name), not a hash. starsim/solvers.py:585-586. Names 'ab' and 'ba' collide, as do many ordinary names. Whole-run independent diseases with these names consequently obtain identical random trajectories (prior-session verified, normal JIT rerun pending); even without whole-run reproduction, identical seed inputs follow directly from code. It is module-level, not transition-level as docs promise. Use stable versioned full-path derivation and independent transition streams. Prioritize this together with shared-kernel RNG as one architectural randomness fix, not two patches.

P1: Run identity omits model modifiers and CRN settings.
References: starsim/sim.py:1272-1288 hashes modules/networks/stratify and interventions, while to_spec separately emits modifiers at1315; config at1317-1322 omits crn. Contradicts design/11-random.md:80-84. Switching ss.options.context(crn=True/False) produced identical five-part hash. Thus different effects can look like same run. Resolve execution options into immutable per-run config, serialize and hash all behavior-affecting inputs, and derive identity from canonical spec once instead of rebuilding subsets by hand. Source-version provenance can remain a best-effort supplement.

P1/P2: check_dt fails for supported per-module timesteps and can report false zero changes.
starsim/sim.py:445-490 assumes every result uses sim.t: coarse n=sim.t.npts-1 and reshape(n,factor). Sim(Disease(...,dt=days(2)),solver='ode',unit='day',dt=1,stop=8,total_pop=100).check_dt(output=True) raises ValueError cannot reshape array of size4 into shape(8,2). Also scale==0 assigns change=0 even when fine result is nonzero; skipping cumulative and multidimensional results contradicts 'every result'. Align stock/flow data on actual time coordinates, adjust all relevant declared clocks or state precisely which clocks change, report absolute and relative errors with zero-safe scale. For stochastic runs, one coarse/fine realization does not identify discretization error; offer ensemble uncertainty or clearly label it as an unreplicated sensitivity check. Validate factor as positive integer >1.

P1 contract gap: gillespie freezes smooth time-varying rates on four pieces per output timestep.
starsim/kernels.py:117,744-745,870; starsim/solvers.py:3097-3104. A note is emitted at solvers.py:1084-1085, so this is disclosed; do NOT claim silent freezing. However design/04-parameters.md:81 and best-practices/stochastic-compartmental.md:87-93 require inhomogeneous Poisson treatment, and the public solver is repeatedly called exact. design/12-backends.md:262 is internally weaker ('not acceptable without a note'). A note cannot make this exact, and accuracy depends on output dt. Implement integrated hazards/thinning with controlled approximation for unsupported rates, or expose this solver under a distinct approximate name and refuse exact gillespie when unsupported. Decouple sampling/output interval from numerical accuracy.

P2 UX/design: numeric calendar-year heuristic changes the meaning of stop based on magnitude.
starsim/time.py:2250-2258 uses >=1900, including ss.years durations. Verified Sim(...,stop=1800).init yields days0..1800 with unit=None, whereas stop=2000 yields calendar1950..2000 with unit='year'. This is a dangerous semantic discontinuity during exploratory duration changes. Require explicit calendar dates/date strings for calendar time; keep bare numbers consistently durations in chosen unit. Explicitly distinguish date from duration even if this breaks historical convenience.

DESIGN: Replace statistically impossible/unreliable universal conformance claims.
design/18-conformance.md:55 demands stochastic mean within2MCSE of ODE at EVERY output time for population>=100000. A nonlinear CTMC expectation is not generally its deterministic ODE (E[SI] differs from E[S]E[I]); increasing replicate count reduces MCSE around the stochastic expectation, exposing rather than curing finite-population discrepancy. Multiple pointwise2MCSE assertions also have uncontrolled familywise false-failure probability. Analogous KS p>=.01 is not an equivalence test. Use analytic linear-process fixtures, numerical error tolerances plus separately justified finite-population bias, simultaneous confidence bands or calibrated global statistics, and explicit equivalence margins for approximation comparisons. Distinguish stochastic law agreement, deterministic limit, and numerical convergence.

DESIGN: The blanket O(dt) ordering-effect claim needs assumptions.
best-practices/composition-and-effects.md:288-299 and design/10-execution.md:15 treat ordering effects as always vanishing with dt. This can hold for consistent splitting of regular continuous dynamics. Threshold policies, daily delivery/event schedules, instantaneous discrete actions, or genuinely noncommuting interventions need not satisfy it. Smaller dt is not evidence that a chosen module order is epidemiologically correct. Specify explicit within-time event ordering and supported convergence assumptions; show user-facing causal order in describe/loop. Preserve deliberate ordered composition, but frame it as model semantics rather than a universal numerical guarantee.

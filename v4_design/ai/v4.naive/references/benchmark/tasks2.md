# Modeling tasks, set 2

Use Starsim 4 (`import starsim as ss`). Time is in days unless stated. "Seeds" are people infectious at the start. Unless stated, use a deterministic run if Starsim offers one. Report each answer as a single number (or date).

- H1. An SIR epidemic in a population of 1,000,000 that mixes at random, with R0 = 2.5, a 7-day mean infectious period, and 100 seeds on 2025-03-01. 10% of the population is under 5, 20% is 5 to 14, and 70% is 15 or over. On 2025-06-01, 80% of children under 5 are vaccinated with a vaccine that fully protects everyone who gets it. How many infections does the campaign avert by 2025-12-31?
- H2. An SIR epidemic in a population of 100,000 with R0 = 2.5, a 7-day mean infectious period, 10 seeds, and a 2% case fatality ratio (2% of infectious people die instead of recovering, after the same time). How many people die within 365 days?
- H3. An SIR epidemic in a population of 1,000,000 starting on 2025-01-01, with R0 = 2, a 5-day mean infectious period, and 10 seeds. On what calendar date are daily new infections highest?
- H4. A stochastic SIR epidemic in a population of 10,000 with R0 = 2, a 7-day mean infectious period, and 10 seeds. Give a 90% interval (5th and 95th percentiles) for the fraction of the population ever infected. (Two numbers.)
- H5. An SEIR epidemic in a population of 1,000,000: R0 = 3, a mean latent period of 5 days, a mean infectious period of 7 days, and 100 seeds (infectious, not latent). On what day is the number of infectious people highest?
- H6. An SIR epidemic with R0 = 2, a 7-day infectious period, a population of 1,000,000, and 100 seeds. On day 0, 30% of the population receives a vaccine that fully protects 90% of recipients and does nothing for the rest. How many infections does the vaccine avert over 365 days?

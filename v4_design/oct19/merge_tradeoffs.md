# Merging v4-rs-port into v4-rs-ck: tradeoffs

Written 2026-10-20 while merging `origin/v4-rs-port` (Robyn's replay of `v4-rs` onto v4, tip `60519d39`) into `v4-rs-ck` (v4 at `92902b20`). Where the port was clearly better it was adopted with no note. Each entry below is a place where the two sides did the same thing differently, or one side's feature cuts across the other's design; it says what was kept, and what the other side gave up.

## Kept v4's version

- **A split over several dimensions.** Both sides fixed the same bug (a split over age and another dimension replaced the age split). v4 (TL4) reads the table as the share at each combination of levels: a split also given to one of the dimensions must agree with the table's totals, or `ss.InconsistentDimension` is raised, and a table over age whose rows each sum to 1 is read as the share within each age group. The port read every such table as the share of the stratified dimension within each level of the others, so the other dimensions keep a split given to them, with a note when the table's totals differ. The port's rule needs no special case for age and never raises, but it silently reinterprets a table that is a joint distribution; v4's is the rule in spec 05 and in the TL4 fix. Kept v4's, in the code and in spec 05.
- **Transmission over networks with acts.** The port made a bare `beta` on a network with acts a probability per act, compounded over the acts in `net_beta()` (its log, #2). v4's per-act transmission design (`transmit_acts()`, hazards per act in each direction, `ss.peract`) replaced that code path, so the port's `typed_beta()` was dropped.
- **`ss.Pregnancy`.** The port's last commit made `ss.Pregnancy` the declared module (`NP` and `P`, conception, abortion, and losses by gestational age, under any solver) and renamed v4's module written as code `ss.LegacyPregnancy`. On the port, the declared module does not have yet what the coded one gives: unborn agents, `ss.PrenatalNet` and `ss.BreastfeedingNet`, burn-in, and v4's fixes from the TLO review (TL23 to TL31), which v4's tests, the MNCH library, and the household network rely on. Those come with the later `v4-rs` work (Tasks 5 and 6 of the port plan: fetal levels, lactation, burn-in). So `ss.Pregnancy` stays the coded module, and the declared one is `ss.pregnancy.Pregnancy`, with its tests (`test_pregnancy.py`). The rename can be made when the declared module has the rest.
- **An immediate split between several destinations** (`ss.progression('A -> B | C', p=[0.3, None])` with no rate or duration). The port runs it as a split on entry. v4 raises `ss.UndrivenTransition`, which says to write one transition per destination, as you decided in the care cascade's decision 17 (option a, with b, the port's behavior, if it comes up a lot). Kept v4's; the port's one test of it now uses a branch per destination. `ss.stay` with one other destination (`ss.Transition(LL, [T, ss.stay], p=[0.35, None])`) still works as the port has it.
- **Values by sex with no sex stratification.** The port read `ss.by(sex=[f, m])` by position from `people.female` as agents; v4 raises `ss.MissingDimension` for values by position and reads them by name (`ss.by(sex=dict(female=f, male=m))`). Kept v4's; the port's tests use names, and a distribution by sex given by name now keeps its names (`by_dist()`).
- **Lazy references to people.** The port's tests wrote `ss.People.female`; v4 has `ss.ppl.female` (your decision B), so they use that.

## Kept the port's version

- **A dwell time with a distribution for each level** (`dur=ss.by(sex=dict(female=ss.lognorm_ex(...), male=...))`). v4's `dwell_by()` handled only an `ss.by` of distributions, and as compartments gave every group one number of Erlang stages from the mean and standard deviation over all groups. The port's `by_dist()` and `varying_dwell()` also take a distribution whose parameters are `ss.by` values or functions, and give each group its own number of stages (ragged stages), so each group's dwell keeps its own shape. The port's version raises `ss.CapabilityError` for an `ss.by` of distributions over two dimensions, which v4's allowed as agents.

- **When agents schedule an exit** (the port's `24eedcd9`, "Schedule exits at entry, as agents", with the late draws and the same-step cascade built on it). The port draws a dwell when a person enters a state, rather than at the next step, and the events, clocks, and triggers it adds rely on that. It moves the agents' results at a step as long as the dwell, which falls under your decision 16 (each method keeps its natural reading). For an SIS with a dwell of a year (`beta=ss.peryear(2)`, `dur=ss.years(1)`, 20,000 agents, 2000 to 2030, mean over seeds 1 to 3), the mean number infected is 9,489 on v4 and 7,518 on the port and the merge, against 9,110 for the ODE. At a step of 3 months it is 9,856 against 9,614 (ODE 9,201), and at a month 9,493 against 9,471 (ODE 9,222). Kept the port's, since its features need it. `test_deliveries.py::test_memory`, which compares agents and the ODE at a step of a year, now uses a step of half a year (agents against ODE retests: 1.004; at a year, 0.94).

## Both kept, overlapping

- **Time spent in a state.** v4's given-dwell design has `P.dwell` (the time each person has spent in `P`, in a rate or `p=` of an exit out of `P`). The port has clocks: `X.time`, `(A | B).time`, and `X.progress`, readable from any module and in conditions. Both are kept; they overlap for the time in one state read by its own exits. Choosing one spelling (or making `P.dwell` the same object as `P.time`) is open.
- **Events.** Both sides added conditions for "what just happened to people", with the same internal name (`op='event'`). v4's are the outcomes of deliveries and products (`screening.positive`), `ss.Event` arrays, and transitions as events; the port's are a branch of a transition (`tr.to(B)`), entering or leaving a state or a set of states (`X.enter`, `(A | B).exit`), `ss.child()`, and `tr.uids`. They answer different questions, so both are kept, with the port's under their own leaf (`op='move'`) so that v4's lineage and memory code is not confused by them.

## The delivery layer: the port's features in v4's spelling

As you chose, v4's cascade (peers linked by events, `who=`/`what=`, `.ever`/`.count`/`.since`) is the base, and the port's features were rebuilt on it, in the spelling of the care cascade's third draft where it has one. Its decisions 18 to 23 are still open, so each choice below that leans on a recommendation there is yours to change.

- **Follow-ups are steps.** `then=`, lists in `on_positive=`, `on_result=`, and `on_fail=` give an error that says what to write: a step with `who=` the delivery (`ss.vaccinate(vx, who=visit)`), one of its results (`who=screening.hsil`), or its failure (`who=clinic.failed`). Failure as a transition is `ss.Transition(P + Y + C, A, on=drug.failed)`, which moves those people when the outcome happens, under every solver (with `p=`, the share who move); the port's `on_fail=` transition worked as compartments through the treatment itself. Lost: a follow-up that is a function of `(sim, uids)` (the port's `then=lambda sim, uids: ...`); `count_events=True` and `events_df()` replace its use in the tests.
- **A shared capacity is `ss.Resource`**, given to steps as `uses=resource` or `uses={resource: amount}` (the draft's decisions 18 and 19, recommendation a), not the port's `ss.Capacity` as `capacity=`. It is allocated as the port allocated it: the steps that run on their own are served jointly at the first of them, by class (`priority=` a number, 0 first), pro rata within a class. Results are `available`, `used`, and `fraction_used` (the port's `unused` is `available - used`). Not built: footprints over a resource's own dimension (officer types), `level=`, `stock=` and `not_done`, `record=`, `sim.new(resources='unlimited')`.
- **`priority=` follows the draft:** a condition, or a list of them, served in order; otherwise a score, served highest first (an expression such as `ss.ppl.age` or `-hiv.cd4`, or a function of `(sim, uids)`). The port served the lowest key first and had `ss.desc()` for highest first; `ss.desc()` is gone (write `-x` or the score itself), and the port's tests changed accordingly (`lambda sim, uids: -uids` for lowest UID first).
- **`queue=`**: as on the port, with the draft's names: those who leave unserved at the end are the outcome `never_ran` (an event and a result); `waited` is the port's `wait`; `dropped` is `lost_capacity` (both for a step with no queue and, with one, for `never_ran`), as v4 already called it.
- **`target=`**: as on the port, with `above=` for the port's `remove=` (`above=tx.dropout`, a transition; `'stop'`, which takes a held product away or moves people back along a `move=` arrow; or a rate, new), and the draft's results: `holding` (the port's `n_end`), `target`, `stopped` (`removed`), and `shortfall`. `ss.share(x, among=...)` is kept rather than the draft's "a number below 1 is a share", which would make the same keyword mean two things. The port's `of=` (count something else) is dropped. A target is counted among everyone holding the product (as on the port), not among those who meet `who=` (as the draft says).
- **Memory and doses.** The port's delivery memory (`d.since`, `d.n`, `p.held` with its own levels as compartments) is v4's memory of events (`.since`, `.count`, `.ever`); `vx.held` is kept as a condition on holding. `repeat=` is dropped: `who=... & ~drug.ever` is once ever, and `who=... & (test.since >= ss.years(5))` is not within five years. `dose=` is dropped: a delivery of a product with `doses=` gives each holder their next dose, so a second dose is a step for those who had the first (`who=vx.ever & (vx.since >= ss.months(6))`). Routine delivery once per spell of being eligible (the port's `ss.Delivery.spells`, pending a decision, default on) is kept; it is the draft's re-arming (MA15).
- **Tests with several results** are `ss.Diagnostic(results=dict(cancer=..., hsil=..., normal=None))`, most severe first with the last `None`, as the draft has it; the port's `ss.Test(outcomes=dict(normal=None, ...))` had the reverse order. Each result is an event and a result of its own name; the port's `diagnosed` (v4's `positive`) and its `outcome` result with parts by result (`res.prog.outcome.select(result='hsil')`) are not added. The port's `combine=` and delays by result are kept.
- **Treatments of several transitions or diseases** (`moves=`) keep v4's outcome names: the port's `overtreated` is `unneeded`. With several diseases, `unneeded` is counted for each (treated for CT and not needing it, though cured of NG), and the total is their sum, as the port counted it; as agents the event `unneeded` is those in no source of any. As compartments, a treatment of several diseases moves people jointly (the port's code), and its outcomes are results but not events, so a step that reads them raises `ss.CapabilityError`.
- **Numbers are people** (`ss.results.people_now`, pending a decision) is adopted for deliveries as on the port: results are written in people, and capacities and targets are people.

## Bugs in v4 that the port's tests found

Fixed as part of the merge, each covered by the port's test that found it.

- A subclass of a declared disease that adds a transition whose rate is a function (`class Old(BV): recur = ss.progression(..., rate=f)`) raised `DuplicateDeclaration` ("two members are both named rate"): a parameter named after its slot in the parent class was not renamed when the subclass's clashed with it (`graph.py`).
- In a compartmental run, when a product's memory moved the people a delivery reached, the people of an outcome of that delivery (within them) moved only in proportion, so a step triggered by the outcome found about 5% fewer people than the outcome had (HPV screen and treat: 7,043 against 7,398). `carry_events()` now moves all of them (`interventions.py`).
- `test_demographics.py::test_declared_newborns` compared the people in a declared disease's states with `n_alive`, which since TL26 leaves out the unborn of `ss.Pregnancy`, who are in the states; the test now counts them.

## Tests changed to pass

- `test_deliveries.py::test_coinfection`: syphilis infections with HIV coinfection more than 5% above without, now more than without (about 11% more over 10 seeds, 2% for seed 1 since the agents changes), as the port had it.
- `test_networks.py::test_route_modifiers`: the agents' cut by a route modifier over 8 seeds within 15% of the ODE's, rather than over 4 seeds within 10% (0.22 to 0.28 over 4 seeds at a time, against the ODE's 0.26).
- `test_deliveries.py::test_memory`: at a step of half a year (see "When agents schedule an exit").
- `test_pregnancy.py::test_states`: `p_loss` per month (v4's is per week of pregnancy), and losses of women who conceived again in the same step left out of the gestational ages.
- `baseline.yaml` regenerated (the agents changes move random streams: SIR prevalence 0.302 to 0.292, SIS 0.518 to 0.524).

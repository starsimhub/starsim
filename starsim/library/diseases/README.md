# Library diseases

Example disease models, as `ssl.diseases.X` (and also as `ssl.X`); see the library's README for each.

- `agents.py`: Starsim 3's SIR, SEIR, and SIS, written as code
- `cholera.py`: cholera, with direct and environmental (waterborne) transmission
- `covid.py`: COVID-19, as a SECIR model
- `dengue.py`: dengue, with several serotypes and a mosquito vector
- `ebola.py`: Ebola, with severe disease and transmission from unburied bodies
- `hiv.py`: HIV, with CD4 counts, plus an ART intervention and a CD4 analyzer
- `influenza.py`: seasonal influenza, after Baguelin et al. (2013): by age, with asymptomatic infection, hospitalization and death, and yearly vaccination
- `malaria.py`: malaria, as the Ross-Macdonald model and as the Garki model of immunity
- `measles.py`: measles by age, with maternal immunity, deaths by age, and two routine doses of vaccine
- `ncd.py`: an example non-communicable disease, written as code
- `pertussis.py`: pertussis (whooping cough), as an SEIRWS model with immune boosting, by age, with the DTP series
- `polio.py`: polio, with lasting protection from paralysis, gut immunity that wanes, and routine OPV and IPV
- `rsv.py`: respiratory syncytial virus (RSV), after Pitzer et al. (2015): repeated, milder infections by age, hospitalization, and maternal vaccine and nirsevimab
- `sti.py`: syphilis and gonorrhea
- `tb.py`: tuberculosis, as the LSHTM spectrum-of-disease model
- `typhoid.py`: typhoid, after Pitzer et al. (2014): clinical and subclinical infections, chronic carriers, water-borne spread, and typhoid conjugate vaccine
